About the desk

A desk, not a billboard.

Broadcastio started as a shared note between people who publish beside other jobs. The site is the part we were willing to show.

The table in the photograph is the right scale. Laptops open, a pair of headphones, no stage. Pieces ship when someone has used the thing across a week that includes a bad room and a real publish button.

We file work on five desks: product reviews, platform guides, studio craft, creator tools, and industry. A microphone recommendation and a note about where a show should live can sit in the same volume. They answer different questions.

Write the desk if a room we described is not the room you have.

Method

How a piece gets published.

  1. Gear stays through at least two recording sessions, not an unboxing.
  2. We write down the room, the distance, and the software.
  3. Platform pieces use the same episode published more than one way, when the format allows.
  4. Scores are not for sale. A partnership, if one ever appears, will be labeled and will not move a verdict.
